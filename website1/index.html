GPU / BUILT INTO THE WORKFLOW

More time shaping
the next frame.

Work through keys, lens fixes and 3D scenes with GPU rendering paths built on wgpu: Vulkan on Linux and Metal on macOS. Supported results stay on the GPU between operations and during cached playback.

01 / COMPOSITE

From the key to the final merge.

GPU paths cover Blur, Keyer, lens distortion and motion-blurred transforms. Color, merge, channel and resize nodes also use the GPU when their inputs are already there.

02 / BUILD IN 3D

Light it. Move it. See it together.

Scanline rendering and shaded 3D previews have GPU paths for meshes, shadows, motion blur, Gaussian splats and large particle sprites.

GPU where it helps. CPU fallback where needed. Coverage depends on the operation, hardware and memory budget. Toggle acceleration in Preferences and check your setup with arclight gpu --check. Features described here reflect the development source; available release builds may differ.

01 / COMPOSITING ENGINE

Built for the whole shot

Keep bright highlights, soft edges and render passes intact as you combine live action and CG. A 32-bit float, linear-light pipeline gives small productions room to refine the image.

32bit

Float RGBA

Every pixel, every node. Values above 1.0 survive until you decide otherwise.

linear

Linear light

Composite in linear light for natural blends, with sRGB conversions at supported image I/O boundaries.

premult

Associated alpha

Premultiplied throughout — blurs and merges fade cleanly with no dark fringing.

DAG

Cached graph

Each node cached by a hash of op, params and inputs. Edit one; only downstream re-renders.

∞cores

Parallel

Per-pixel work spread across every core with Rayon; a frame cache reuses results as you scrub.

200undo

Safe to explore

Every graph edit is undoable. A whole slider drag is one step.

FOR INDIE VFX ARTISTS

Small team. Bigger scenes.

From a solo passion project to a short-film crew, build effects around the footage you have and the story you want to tell.

2D COMPOSITING

Make the screen replacement stick.

Track the plate, link a Corner Pin, roto the foreground and match the insert with grading and lens distortion. Keep the moving parts connected when the shot changes.

GEOMETRY NODES

Dress a world without placing every rock.

Scatter points over surfaces or inside volumes. Instance meshes, cards or splat captures, then vary scale and color with attributes, noise, falloffs and image masks.

3D + GAUSSIAN SPLATS

Bring a captured location into the shot.

Import an existing splat capture, crop stray points and grade it alongside CG. Use camera tracking, holdouts and depth-aware rendering to combine the scene with your plate.

03 / NODE LIBRARY

The arsenal

Build a shot from readable steps: key, roto, track, grade, scatter, light and merge. Animate numeric controls and use masks on supported image nodes.

04 / CREATIVE TOOLKIT

One graph. More possibilities.

Replace a screen, extend a set or bring a captured location into a short film. Connect the tools in one graph and keep refining each part of the shot.

05 / SCRIPTING & AUTOMATION

Creative control, programmable.

Python, MCP for AI agents, native plugins and live formulas all drive one engine API. Scripts can even attach to the running app — their edits land in your undo history.

06 / MEDIA & FORMATS

Pipeline-ready I/O

In

  • EXR 32-bit float, every pass as a layer, data windows
  • DPX 8/10/12/16-bit & float, log → linear via Cineon
  • TIFF · TGA · WebP · PNG · JPEG · HDR
  • Video mp4 · mov · mkv · mxf · webm (via ffmpeg, 10-bit+ kept at 16-bit)
  • Sequences drop one frame, get shot.####.exr
  • 3D Alembic .abc · splats .ply .splat .spz

Out

  • ProRes 422 HQ · 4444 + alpha
  • DNxHR HQX · 444 (10-bit)
  • HEVC · AV1 10-bit · H.264 · VP9 (+ alpha)
  • DPX 10-bit log, video, or 32-bit float
  • EXR with depth, P, N, uv, motion, mattes, diffuse…
  • Audio carried through to .mov / .mp4 in sync
07 / ROADMAP

The next chapter

The goal: a capable compositing workspace for independent films, music videos and ambitious personal projects. These directions are future work, with no promised delivery date.

  1. More room for large plates — tiled evaluation and region-of-interest processing are roadmap ideas
  2. Viewer color management — OCIO / GPU display transforms are roadmap ideas
  3. A broader indie workflow — continued development of the shared 2D, 3D and procedural geometry toolset
